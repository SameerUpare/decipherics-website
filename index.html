01 / Why this lab exists

Learn. Experiment. Research. Contribute.

A shared space for understanding financial-crime technology.

DECiPHERiCS is an independent learning, research and experimentation lab focused on the technology behind financial-crime detection. It is designed to move beyond theoretical discussions and explore how financial-crime systems actually work — from sanctions data engineering and entity resolution to payment screening, machine learning, graph analytics and transaction monitoring.

The objective is to create a practical space where practitioners, technology professionals, researchers and learners can build understanding, test ideas, examine limitations and share what they discover.

01

Learn

Understand concepts, architectures, algorithms and the data foundations behind AML systems.

02

Experiment

Build prototypes, test approaches and compare methods using controlled or synthetic datasets.

03

Research

Investigate emerging techniques across AI, NLP, machine learning and graph analytics.

04

Contribute

Share ideas, challenge findings, suggest experiments and help expand the collective knowledge base.

Built to be challenged: Suggest a research question, question an assumption, share a use case or point out where an experiment falls short. Critical feedback is part of the research process.

04 / Our approach

From question to evidence.

The lab follows an experiment-led approach: understand the problem, build a testable method, measure the outcome and document what the evidence does—and does not—show.

STEP 01

Frame the problem

Define the financial-crime use case, data assumptions and decision objective.

STEP 02

Build the experiment

Create a prototype, dataset or workflow that makes the hypothesis testable.

STEP 03

Measure & challenge

Evaluate performance, edge cases, operational trade-offs and failure modes.

STEP 04

Explain the findings

Publish the method, results, limitations and lessons for further investigation.
